How It Works

From Stethoscope to
Medicine Bag in Seconds

Your prescription reaches the pharmacist before your patient reaches the stairs. Here's the magic, step by step.

1

Patient Scans QR

Walks in, scans the QR code on the wall. Automatically added to the live queue. Takes 3 seconds.

2

Doctor Consults

See the queue in real time. Call next patient with one tap. Access full history. Generate prescription in seconds.

3

Rx Sent Instantly

Prescription PDF lands on patient's WhatsApp and simultaneously pings the local pharmacist's portal. Zero delay.

4

Medicines Ready

Pharmacist packs the order while the patient walks downstairs. Pick up and go. The loop is closed.
